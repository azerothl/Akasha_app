          <p>For <strong>Azure OpenAI</strong>, set <code>api_key_ref</code> and <code>base_url</code> (resource endpoint); the <strong>deployment name</strong> is the <code>model</code> in the route entry.</p>

          <h3><code>primary.config</code> / route <code>config</code> (per model)</h3>
          <p>Optional JSON/YAML object merged into completion requests: <code>temperature</code>, <code>top_p</code>, <code>top_k</code>, <code>frequency_penalty</code>, <code>presence_penalty</code>, <code>repeat_penalty</code>, <code>num_ctx</code>, <code>num_gpu</code>, <code>max_tokens</code> (legacy alias <code>max_token</code>), <code>thinking_level</code> (string). Large integers are clamped to 32-bit limits.</p>
