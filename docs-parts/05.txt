          <h2>What's new in v0.10.0</h2>
          <p>Your assistant can live on the machine — and talk to a small companion on the LAN.</p>
          <p>Bind the daemon so an ESP32 Companion can find it, pair, and use voice without leaving your network (<code>AKASHA_BIND</code>, <code>/api/companion/*</code>).</p>
          <p>First use can download and set up a local model: wizard, calibration, then you stay embedded when you want. On mid-size Ollama models, prompts stay compact and context defaults to 8k.</p>
