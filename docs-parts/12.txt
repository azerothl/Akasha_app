dio@0.10.0</code> — serves the cockpit on <code>http://127.0.0.1:5178</code> and proxies <code>/api</code> to the daemon (default port <strong>3876</strong>). Override with <code>VITE_DAEMON_URL</code> or <code>AKASHA_STUDIO_PORT</code>.</p>

          <p><code>spawned</code> → <code>ready</code> → <code>running</code> → <code>blocked</code> | <code>completed</code> | <code>failed</code> | <code>stopped</code></p>
          <h3>Events</h3>
          <p>Structured events on <code>GET /api/tasks/:id/events</code>: <code>studio_worker_state_changed</code>, <code>studio_subtask_assigned</code>, <code>studio_subtask_completed</code>, <code>studio_subtask_failed</code>, <code>studio_conflict_notice</code>.</p>
          <h3>Limits</h3>
          <p>Respect <code>AKASHA_STUDIO_MAX_PARALLEL_OPS</code>; swarm is opt-in via task context. All writes stay inside the project disk root.</p>
