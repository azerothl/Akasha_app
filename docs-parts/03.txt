          <h2>Introduction</h2>
          <p>
            <strong>Akasha</strong> is a privacy-first personal assistant built around a <strong>long-running daemon</strong>.
            With the <strong>embedded model</strong>, you get answers without signing up or calling the cloud.
            You can add <strong>Ollama</strong> or <strong>OpenAI / OpenRouter</strong> when you need larger models — keys stay in your local vault.
          </p>
          <p>
            Skills extend what the agent can do (calculator, custom workflows, …). Optional channels (Telegram, Slack, Discord) let the same daemon serve multiple interfaces.
          </p>
